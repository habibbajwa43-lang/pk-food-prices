# 7. Limitations and next steps

This dataset shows how recorded prices changed over time and across cities, but it cannot explain why prices changed or how much individual households actually paid. The biggest risks are the 301-day gap in the national time series, the limited coverage of 17 urban centres, and the lack of information about how PBS selected its markets. Next, I would download and check the remaining PBS Report files, investigate whether the missing period can be filled, and test forecasts on genuinely unseen weeks.
